SUBJECT-SPECIFIC STRATEGY
Moral Science (3 Oct)
Two units — Peer Pressure (U4) and Positive Emotions (U5) + School Diary rules. Treat this as pure memorization. Make 1-page summary per unit. Revise 3 times before exam day.
Maths (5 Oct)
Biggest risk: Factorization + Special Products gaps + Discount. Do these first. Venn Diagrams: get from class or ask teacher this week. Rational Numbers + Sets + Squares/Cubes = already done, just revise. Do at least 5 numericals per weak chapter.
Physics (6 Oct) — Numericals Fix
Every numerical: write unit conversions at the top FIRST, always. km→m, g→kg, etc. before touching the formula. Do this without fail for 5 days and it becomes automatic. Light Energy ray diagrams are also in the project — double benefit.
Computer (7 Oct)
Strong subject. Only risk: book definitions. Write them down once per chapter — writing encodes better than reading. One session should be enough.
Chemistry (8 Oct)
3 chapters completely undone. Hydrogen, Water, Chemical Reactions — prioritize in Week 1. Physical + Chemical Changes already done. Chemical Reactions: focus on word equations and types (combination, decomposition, etc.).
Language (9 Oct)
Easy. Make a single page: all definitions + vocabulary list. Read it twice before exam. Don't over-prepare here — spend that time on Bio or History.
Biology (10 Oct) — HIGHEST PRIORITY
Fully undone. 3 chapters for 2nd term: Transportation in Plants, Endocrine System, Reproduction in Plants. Start from Day 1. Read chapter → make short notes → recall without looking. One chapter per day in Week 1.
History/Civics (12 Oct)
Notes done but not understood. Don't just re-read — understand the flow. 1857 Uprising: causes → events → result. Socio-Religious Reforms: which reformer did what. Freedom Struggle: key events timeline. Civics: Legislature structure + UN. Make a one-page timeline.
Geography (13 Oct)
Fully undone. 4 chapters: Disasters, India Human Resources, Geographical Features, Climate. These are largely factual — read once, note key facts, revise. Maps if needed. Not conceptually hard — just needs reading time.
Hindi (14 Oct)
Notes done. Hindi I = grammar (sandhi, samas, etc.) — revise definitions and examples. Hindi II = poems + stories — read each one, understand the theme, remember author names. Don't ignore this one late.
Literature (15 Oct)
Notes done, chapters unread. For each Magic Carpet story: theme, main character, 2 key events. For Little Women chapters: character names, what happens, any quotes your teacher highlighted. Poems: figure of speech examples, central idea.
